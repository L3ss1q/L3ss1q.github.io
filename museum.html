CURATOR'S NOTE

Computer history isn't a straight line from one “first computer” to the next. Telegraph networks helped normalize fast long-distance messaging; mathematical theories, punched-card systems, electronic circuits, software, and shared networks each contributed different pieces. Many breakthroughs were developed by teams, and some milestones happened over several years.

This is a curated introduction, not a complete inventory. Some dates are approximate or refer to a key public milestone rather than the very first experiment.
